Privacy Policy

WatchVerse · Last updated: 14 October 2026

WatchVerse is a movie and TV tracking app. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can see, correct, or delete it.

It is written to meet the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller (veri sorumlusu) for WatchVerse is the app's developer, reachable at gulfemkupeli@gmail.com. Any request about your personal data can be sent to that address.

2. What we collect

WatchVerse has no advertising and does not sell or share your data with advertisers. It uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics — see “Technical data” below and section 5 for what those collect. Everything else below is data you create by using the app.

Account data

  • Email address — used as your login identifier.
  • Password — handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication. It is stored as a salted hash on Google's systems; the app never receives or stores your plain password.
  • Display name, profile photo, profile background and the “If I were a movie/TV character” line — optional, and only if you add them.
  • Account creation date.

Library and viewing data

  • Movies and shows you mark as To Watch, Watched or favourite, and the up-to-three films you showcase on your profile.
  • Star ratings you give.
  • Diary entries: the date you watched something, plus any note you write.
  • Custom lists you create, and other users' public lists you follow.
  • Per-episode watched marks and private episode notes.
  • Your answers to the post-watch “How was it?” poll (rating, platform, emotion, favourite character).
  • Your results in the in-app games (the daily movie game, Frames, Title Wordle, Plot and Who’s the Actor?). Higher or Lower best scores stay on your device only.

Social data

  • Who you follow, and who follows you.
  • Users you have blocked.
  • Comments you post on titles (including any photo or GIF you attach), and likes you give to comments.
  • “What should we watch?” group polls you create or are invited to, and your votes in them.
  • Content reports you submit for moderation.

Technical data

  • Push notification token — a device identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, stored on your profile while you are signed in on a device where you allowed notifications. It is removed from your profile when you sign out. Your notification on/off choices from Settings are stored on your profile too. WatchVerse uses them to send the notifications you leave switched on: new episodes of shows you track airing today or tomorrow, movies on your watchlist released today, a daily digest of friends’ activity, and shared-watch matches.
  • Google Firebase records standard technical information such as IP address and timestamps when the app connects to its servers.
  • App usage data (Firebase Analytics) — which screens you open and basic navigation events, so we can see which parts of the app are actually used. This is not linked to your account or email — Firebase assigns it an anonymous device-installation identifier only.
  • Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — technical details captured automatically if the app crashes (device model, OS version, and the point in the code where it failed), so bugs can be fixed. Also not linked to your account.

Payment data (WatchVerse Pro and Patron)

  • WatchVerse offers optional Pro and Patron subscriptions, purchased entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit) system. Your payment card details are handled by Apple and are never seen, received, or stored by WatchVerse.
  • The app only learns which subscription tier StoreKit reports for your Apple ID, and stores that on your device — not on our servers and not on your WatchVerse account — so that subscriber features unlock. No purchase history, billing address, or card information reaches us.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, your photos beyond a profile picture or comment image you deliberately choose, or your device's advertising identifier.

4. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To run your account and library — necessary for performance of the contract between you and the app (KVKK art. 5/2-c; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To provide social features (following, comments, activity feed) — performance of the contract, and your own act of posting.
  • To send push notifications — your explicit consent, given through the operating system's notification permission prompt. You can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.
  • To keep the service safe (handling content reports, blocking abuse) — our legitimate interest in a functioning service (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) and the legitimate-interest basis in KVKK art. 5/2-f.
  • To understand app usage and fix crashes (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) — our legitimate interest in a working, improving app, on the same bases as above.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it to anyone.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and library data — kept until you delete your account.
  • Push notification token — removed from your profile when you sign out, and deleted with your account.
  • Comments — kept until you delete them individually, or until you delete your account.
  • Uploaded images (profile picture, comment images) — replacing your profile picture overwrites the old one, but deleting a comment or your account does not currently delete the image file. Email us and we will delete them.
  • Aggregate poll counts — kept indefinitely, but they contain no identifier and cannot be traced back to you.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself: open Settings → Delete Account in the app. No email request and no waiting period is required. For security, the app asks you to sign in again first if your last sign-in was more than a few minutes ago. Deletion starts immediately and is permanent. If it is interrupted (for example, the app is closed or the connection drops), the account is marked for deletion and the app finishes deleting it the next time you sign in, instead of letting you keep using a partly deleted account. It removes:

  • your profile, display name and account record;
  • your movie and show library, including favourites and ratings;
  • your activity timeline, diary entries and their notes;
  • your custom lists, the lists you follow, and your private per-episode notes and watched marks;
  • your individual poll answers and your game results;
  • every comment you have posted, and your likes on other people's comments;
  • your entry in the follower lists of people you followed;
  • your login credentials.

What in-app deletion does not currently remove:

  • Uploaded images. Your profile picture and any images you uploaded to comments stay in storage after the account is deleted. Email us and we will delete them.
  • Moderation reports. If you have used the report button to flag someone else's comment, that record is kept. It contains the reported comment's ID, your account ID and a timestamp, and it is retained on legitimate-interest grounds so that abuse reports survive the reporter closing their account.
  • Other people's follow lists. Your account ID (not your name or email) can remain in the “following” list of users who followed you.
  • Group polls. “What should we watch?” polls you created (including your display name as creator) or voted in remain visible to their other members.
  • Aggregate post-watch poll percentages. These carry no identifier and cannot be traced back to you.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and, if you are in the EEA or UK, under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is being processed, and request information about it;
  • obtain a copy of the data we hold about you — you can also export your movies, shows, diary entries and lists yourself, free, as CSV files from Settings → Export my data (CSV);
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, and request that third parties to whom it was transferred are informed;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

To exercise any of these, write to gulfemkupeli@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days, as required by KVKK art. 13. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or, in the EEA, with your local supervisory authority.

9. Security

Passwords are never stored by the app itself — authentication is delegated to Google Firebase Authentication. Traffic between the app and its servers is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access to your library is restricted by server-side database rules that check the identity of the signed-in user on every read and write.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Authority without undue delay, as required by KVKK art. 12.

10. Children

WatchVerse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how your data is used, notify you inside the app. Continuing to use WatchVerse after a change means you accept the updated policy.
